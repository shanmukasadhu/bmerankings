Each paper's credit is split evenly among its authors and counted toward the university listed on the paper. Institutions are ranked by the geometric mean of (adjusted count + 1) across the selected areas. Hospitals, companies and non-university institutes get no credit, and medical schools count toward their parent university. There is no hand-curated faculty list, so author lists include students and postdocs, and OpenAlex's automatic author matching can split or merge people.
